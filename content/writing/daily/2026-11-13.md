I think it is time to go back to the basics I had learnt in college. _Build yourself through writing, write something everyday_. Ofcourse not everybody had learnt these exact same "basics". I had enjoyed hanging out with the people who also liked to write and took pride in it. I learnt it from them. I am quite sure I started the #100DaysOfWriting challenge many times and never managed to follow through past 3-4 days. Following through has always been a challenge for me. I would have fits of motivation and start with an overflow of energy and passion and then shortly quit. I want to break that pattern. I want to be slow and steady like the Tortoise. 

I already felt a "sense of self" coming back as I renewed my GitHub website with the help of Cursor. It is now cleaner and better organised. I also plan to write more engineering blogs there. I have a few ideas. 

I haven't written in so long. And I have been using AI to improve my texts so frequently now that it's taking quite some self control to not dump this writing itself into Claude and ask it to "make it sound better". 

I started writing this on 6th Nov, and it is already 13th Nov! I will just publish this blog post now. Will write more soon!
